Status: focused compiler, editor, executable language documentation and comparison sources accepted. Combined regression and measurements are accepted at checkpoint 96.

`target ??= fallback` selects and reads its destination once, then evaluates and commits the fallback only for null or undefined. Zero, false, signed zero, NaN and empty strings keep their existing value. The fallback must fit the declared storage type even when skipped. Indexed bounds precede fallback effects, and permitted destinations are reacquired before commit; failed fallback evaluation leaves the outer assignment uncommitted while completed argument effects remain.

Stable names and direct record fields have absent views within the fallback and resulting views after successful assignment. Writes and conflicting effects invalidate these facts. Copy results are snapshots; owned destinations retain their owners and return readonly aliases. Inferred alias bindings stay readonly even with `let`, and their loans last through the enclosing scope. Explicit clone creates an independent owner.
